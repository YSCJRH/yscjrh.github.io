Concept model / 概念模型

Blue excitation axis Light reaches the sample through the excitation monochromator; the remaining incident beam is shown as stopped, not sent to the detector.

Cyan 90° collection arm Fluorescence is collected from the side path through the emission monochromator before detection.

Blocked excitation path The straight-through excitation direction ends at a stop, so it is not read as detector collection.

Signal output line The fine line after the detector represents a processed display signal, not another optical beam.
