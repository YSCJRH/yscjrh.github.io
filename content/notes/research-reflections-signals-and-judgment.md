# Research Reflections
## When A Fluorescence Signal Becomes Usable

Status: draft working note

### EN

A fluorescence signal does not become useful just because it is measurable.

The harder question is whether the full measurement chain is stable enough to support judgment. Sample condition, instrument behavior, repeatability, and interpretation often move together. If they are treated as separate cleanup steps too early, the result may look cleaner than it really is.

One practical reminder is to ask not only whether a signal is visible, but also which part of the chain is trustworthy enough to carry meaning. If that part is unclear, later algorithmic processing can make the uncertainty harder to see rather than easier to handle.

This is a framing note, not a result claim.

### 中文

一个荧光信号并不会因为“测到了”就自动变得有用。

更困难的问题是，整条测量链是否已经稳定到足以支撑判断。样品状态、仪器行为、重复性和结果解释，往往是一起变化的。如果过早把它们拆成彼此独立的“后处理步骤”，最后得到的东西可能只是看起来更整齐，而不是真的更可靠。

一个实际的提醒是：不要只问信号是否可见，也要问整条链路里到底哪一部分已经足够可信，能够承载意义。如果这件事本身还不清楚，后面的算法处理有时只会让不确定性更难被看见。

这是一条工作中的框架笔记，不是研究结果声明。
