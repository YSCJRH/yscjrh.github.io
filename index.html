Research 研究

Research directions shaped by signals, methods, and instruments. 由信号、方法与仪器共同塑造的研究方向。

This is not a publication list. It is the working frame behind the kinds of analytical questions, methods, and systems that keep recurring here. 这里不是论文或成果清单，而是支撑这些分析问题、方法路径与系统思考的工作框架。

01

Fluorescence Analysis 荧光分析

Question 问题
How can fluorescence signals be turned into information strong enough for analytical judgment? 如何把荧光信号整理成足以支持分析判断的信息？
Approach 路径
Keep measurement conditions, interpretation, and repeatability in one frame instead of treating them as separate steps. 把测量条件、结果解释与重复性放在同一框架里，而不是割裂成彼此独立的步骤。
Why it matters 意义
Reliable signal reading is the base layer for later algorithmic or instrumental decisions. 稳定可靠的信号理解，是后续算法判断与仪器设计的基础层。

02

Fluorescence Analysis × Intelligent Algorithms 荧光分析 × 智能算法

Question 问题
How can difficult fluorescence data become easier to compare, interpret, and use in practice? 如何让复杂的荧光数据在实践中更容易比较、解释和使用？
Approach 路径
Combine algorithmic workflows with domain constraints instead of treating models as detached add-ons. 把算法工作流与领域约束一起考虑，而不是把模型当作脱离问题语境的附加层。
Why it matters 意义
Algorithms become more credible when they clarify decisions rather than hide them. 当算法帮助澄清判断而不是遮蔽判断时，它才真正对分析工作有价值。

03

Fluorescence Instrumentation 荧光仪器

Question 问题
How should sensing, control, method design, and instrument behavior stay aligned as one system? 感知、控制、方法设计与仪器行为，如何作为一个系统保持对齐？
Approach 路径
Treat instruments as working systems rather than isolated hardware components. 把仪器当作有完整工作链条的系统来思考，而不是孤立的硬件部件。
Why it matters 意义
Analytical robustness depends on the full measurement chain, not only on post hoc data handling. 分析工作的稳健性，依赖的是完整测量链条，而不仅是事后数据处理。

ai-visibility-auditor

AnswerLens: CLI-first AI visibility auditor for product websites. AnswerLens：面向产品网站的命令行优先 AI 可见性审计工具。

Explores 探索
How AI discoverability can be audited through GitHub-native, report-driven workflows that write reviewable artifacts. 如何通过 GitHub 原生、报告驱动、可回看产物的工作流来审计 AI discoverability。
Why it matters 为什么重要
It gives teams a way to inspect why AI systems miss, flatten, or misread a site without leaning on dashboard lock-in or UI scraping. 它让团队能够检查 AI 为什么会错过、压平或误读一个网站，而不是依赖 dashboard lock-in 或 UI scraping。

A builder preference for explainable structure, visible evidence, and report-first operational clarity. 它体现出一种构建偏好：偏向可解释结构、可见证据，以及以报告为先的操作清晰性。
