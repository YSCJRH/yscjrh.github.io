Research 研究

A research direction shaped by signals, methods, and instruments. 一条由信号、方法与仪器共同塑造的研究主线。

This is not a list of outputs. It is the working frame behind the questions, tools, and notes collected on this page. 这里不是成果清单，而是支撑这页中问题、工具与笔记的工作框架。

01

Fluorescence Analysis 荧光分析

Focus on how fluorescence signals are measured, interpreted, and made reliable enough to support real decisions. 关注荧光信号如何被测量、解释，并被整理成足以支持真实判断的可靠信息。

02

Fluorescence Analysis × Intelligent Algorithms 荧光分析 × 智能算法

Use algorithmic workflows to make complex fluorescence data more readable, comparable, and operational in practice. 用算法化的工作流让复杂的荧光数据在实践中更易读、更可比较，也更便于转化为可操作的方法。

03

Fluorescence Instrumentation 荧光仪器

Think about instruments as systems where sensing, control, and method design need to stay connected. 把仪器看作一个系统，在其中感知、控制与方法设计需要保持彼此连接。
